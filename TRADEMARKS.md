# PenShare Trademark Policy

The PenShare name, stylized wordmark, logo, pen-nib mark, and related visual identity are referred to here as the "PenShare Marks." They are marks claimed by the PenShare project owner. This policy does not claim that any mark is registered.

The `AGPL-3.0-only` software license does not grant permission to use the PenShare Marks as branding. This trademark policy is separate from the copyright license and does not limit truthful references to the project.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- refer to PenShare accurately in articles, tutorials, reviews, and compatibility statements;
- link to the canonical PenShare repository or website;
- show unaltered screenshots for commentary, education, or reporting; and
- say that another project is "based on PenShare" or "compatible with PenShare" when that statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement.

## Uses that require permission

You need prior written permission to:

- use a PenShare Mark in the name of a product, service, company, domain, package, or social account;
- use the PenShare logo, or a confusingly similar variation, as another project's identity;
- imply that a modified build, hosted service, or commercial offering is official, sponsored, or endorsed by PenShare; or
- use a PenShare Mark on merchandise or in paid advertising as your own brand.

## Forks and redistributed builds

Publicly distributed modified versions must use a distinct product name and visual identity and must not present themselves as an official PenShare release. They may state factually that they are based on PenShare. Copyright notices, license notices, and source-code obligations remain governed by the AGPL.

An unmodified redistribution may identify the software as PenShare, but it must not describe the distributor or build as official unless the project owner has authorized that claim.
